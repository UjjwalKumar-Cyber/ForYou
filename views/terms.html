Terms

ForyoU is being tested as a private memory space for emotional letters, saved moments, and open-later messages.

Private Beta

This site is not a public social network. Access can be changed, paused, or removed during testing so bugs and safety issues can be fixed quickly.

Respectful Use

Do not use ForyoU to harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or send illegal content. The admin can suspend or block accounts that break this rule.

Your Content

You keep responsibility for the messages and media you send. Saved memories and time capsules may be retained longer than normal messages until you delete them or request removal.

Service Limits

The beta is prepared for a small private tester group. Features may change, and temporary downtime can happen while improvements are deployed.
